JiaoKuan Technology, established in Taiwan, represents the convergence of innovation and engineering excellence in the heart of Kaohsiung. Founded and led by Carlos Ma (馬嘉鴻), our company has positioned itself as a trusted partner for businesses seeking cutting-edge technological solutions across multiple domains.

我們位於高雄市鳳山區,扮演傳統工程實務與現代技術需求之間的橋樑,提供完整的解決方案,推動產業升級與數位轉型。
