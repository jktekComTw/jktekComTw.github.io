課號63216
學分3　(選修，學期)
授課教師趙健祥
教師信箱（副教授，已退休，未公開）
人數上限7
對應大綱 · NTU
課名LINEAR CONTROL SYSTEMS
課號ME5220 (522 U5040) · 95-1
學分3

課程簡介 / Description

Continuation of Automatic Control I. Focuses on modern control (state-space methods), with intro to frequency-domain design, robust control and digital control.

涵蓋內容 / Topics

  • (1) State-space design: realization & similarity transform, LTI/LTV solutions, stability, Lyapunov theory, controllability/observability, eigenvalue assignment & LQ control, observer design & Kalman filter
  • (2) Transfer-function design: pole placement via Bezout identity, internal model principle, closed-loop specs in frequency domain
  • (3) Robust control

課名DIGITAL CONTROL SYSTEM
課號ME5247 (522 U5350) · 95-2
學分3

課程簡介 / Description

Basic skills for analysis and control of digital (discrete-time) systems, for students who have taken Automatic Control. Undergraduate level, also suitable for graduate students new to digital control.

涵蓋內容 / Main Topics

  • Z-transform
  • Discrete-time model of sampled system
  • Sampling theorem
  • State-space control
  • Discrete transfer-function control
  • Optimal control & optimal observer

Ref: Franklin/Powell/Workman, Digital Control of Dynamic Systems; Åström & Wittenmark, Computer-Controlled Systems 3e; Goodwin/Graebe/Salgado, Control System Design.
