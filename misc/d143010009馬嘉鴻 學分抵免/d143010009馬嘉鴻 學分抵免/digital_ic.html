課程概述 / DescriptionDescription

此一課程在於介紹數位電路。This course introduces digital circuits.

課程目標 / ObjectivesObjectives

使學生獲得從事數位 IC 設計必須之基礎能力。To give students the fundamental skills required to work in digital IC design.
