Key Takeaway

In $F = F_1 + \dfrac{F_2 - 1}{G_1} + \dfrac{F_3 - 1}{G_1 G_2} + \cdots$, the gain $G_1$ appears in the denominator of every later term. A higher $G_1$ divides away the noise contributions from stages 2, 3, ... making them negligible. Without enough gain, even a low-noise LNA cannot prevent downstream stages from ruining the system noise figure.
